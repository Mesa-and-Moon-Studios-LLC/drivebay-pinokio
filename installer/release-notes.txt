Drivebay 3.37.2: Google and Apple sign-in fix, menu bar, cut and paste, Send to

- Google and Apple sign-in go through an https sign-in page on the Mesa and Moon Firebase domain. Email sign-in is unchanged.
- File, Edit, View and Organize are dropdown menus in a bar that stays at the top.
- Long press (phone) or right-click (computer) a file for Cut, Copy, Paste, Rename, Delete and Send to. A paste bar stays under the menu until you paste or cancel.
- Android 3.37.2 (versionCode 33702). The Windows installer and the server report 3.37.2.
- macOS disk image: Drivebay-<version>-arm64.dmg and Drivebay-<version>-x64.dmg. The disk image is not signed or notarized yet. If macOS says it cannot be opened, right-click Drivebay and choose Open, then click Open. Or run: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Drivebay.app
- macOS tarball: the build is not signed yet. If macOS says it cannot be opened, right-click the folder and choose Open, or run this once on the unpacked folder: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine <folder>
- Linux: drivebay-server-linux-x64.tar.gz and drivebay-server-linux-arm64.tar.gz. Unpack and run bash unix/install.sh.
- version-manifest.json is on the release. The tarballs stay next to the disk images: drivebay-server-darwin-arm64.tar.gz and drivebay-server-darwin-x64.tar.gz.
