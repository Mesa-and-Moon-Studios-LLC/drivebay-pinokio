Drivebay 3.36.1: Connect my phone

- New: right-click the Drivebay tray icon, choose "Connect my phone", and type the 6-digit code from the Drivebay Android app. Your phone shows up in File Explorer as a drive (P:, "Drivebay Phone") and reconnects on its own.
- The code is saved only in Windows Credential Manager. If it stops working, Drivebay stops trying and asks for a new one.
- Fix: the installer now closes every running Drivebay, including older copies, and keeps your saved port.

Needs the Drivebay Android app 3.34.2 or newer with phone-as-drive turned on.
